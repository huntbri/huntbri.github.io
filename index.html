CampusCollective

CampusCollective is a solo UX project exploring how design can encourage more sustainable behavior on campus. I researched student habits and barriers through surveys and observations, then designed and iterated on a mobile app that uses community engagement and friendly competition to motivate change. Through three rounds of prototyping and usability testing with 20 students, I refined features like dashboards, leaderboards, and social aspects to make sustainability feel approachable and rewarding.
